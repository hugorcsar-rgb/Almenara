Four partnership models

01 / 04

For manufacturers who want to integrate the technology into their own luminaires and modules, under their own brand.

02 / 04

Licensed manufacturing

For industrial groups who want to produce the platform in their own region, closer to their customers, under licence from Almenara.

03 / 04

Distribution

For distributors who want to sell the finished modules in their territory to manufacturers, installers and specifiers.

04 / 04

Integration and installation

For engineering firms and installers who carry out retrofit projects in public, industrial and commercial installations.
