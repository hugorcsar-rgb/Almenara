Applications

Where light costs money.

The saving grows with the installed load and the hours it runs. Industry and street lighting are the extreme cases; everything else follows.

Seven sectors

01 / 07

Hospitality

Hotels, restaurants, spas. Warm spectra and faithful rendering in spaces where light is part of what the guest is paying for.

02 / 07

Architecture

Museums, cultural institutions, listed buildings. Light that respects the material, with control over the ultraviolet and infrared that damages it.

03 / 07

Industry

Factories, logistics centres, processing plants. High-bay fittings running continuously, where every watt saved multiplies by thousands of hours.

04 / 07

Retail

Flagship stores, showrooms, galleries. Rendering that holds across the whole range and stays consistent between production batches and over time.

05 / 07

Public infrastructure

Roads, tunnels, civic buildings. Long-life modules sized for minimum maintenance over a twenty-five-year horizon and for European procurement files.

06 / 07

Premium residential

Private houses, yachts, estates. Custom geometries integrated into the architecture, specified by designers who treat light as a material.

07 / 07

Data centres

Server halls and technical aisles. They run above the ambient temperature a standard luminaire is designed for, and heat shortens the life of the gear. Here every watt is saved twice: on the lighting and on the cooling that no longer has to remove it.
