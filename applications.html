03 · Applications

Where it is installed.

The platform adapts to any spectrum, any form factor, any scale. From private residences to public infrastructure.

01

Six sectors.

Hospitality

Five-star hotels, restaurants, spas. Warm spectra, precise rendering, silent operation. Designed for guest-facing environments where light is part of the experience.

Architectural

Museums, cultural institutions, listed buildings. Sympathetic light that respects the material. Conservation-grade UV and IR control.

Industrial

Factories, logistics centres, processing plants. High-bay luminaires with the lowest energy cost per lux on the market. Engineered for continuous duty.

Retail

Flagship stores, showrooms, galleries. Colour rendering that holds across the entire product range. Consistent across batches and over time.

Public infrastructure

Roads, tunnels, civic buildings. Long-life modules engineered for minimum maintenance over a 25-year horizon. Specified in EU energy directives.

Premium residential

Private homes, yachts, estates. Custom geometries integrated with architecture. Specified by interior architects who treat light as a material.
